Solar System / Earth

Our home in space

Earth

The third planet from the Sun is a world of oceans, continents and a life-supporting atmosphere. Earth is the only planet currently known to host life.

PositionThird planet from the Sun
Average distanceAbout 150 million kilometres
Natural moonsOne: the Moon

A world shaped by water

Earth's surface includes liquid oceans, continents and polar ice. The atmosphere helps regulate temperature and protects the surface from some harmful radiation, while the planet's magnetic field deflects many charged particles from the Sun.

A moving planet

Earth rotates once each day, creating day and night, and travels around the Sun in about 365.25 days. The Moon orbits Earth and has a strong influence on ocean tides.
