About Cosmic World

Cosmic World is a friendly starting point for anyone curious about the universe, from the worlds close to home to the tools that help us look farther away.

A guide for curious minds

Space can feel enormous and complex. This guide introduces astronomy in clear, approachable steps: first our planetary neighbourhood, then the wider universe and the ways people study it.

Its images and short explanations are designed to make it easier to follow a topic, discover a new question and keep learning at your own pace.

Choose your next destination

Start with the Sun and planets, then travel further with telescopes, spacecraft and space science. Use the section links on each page to jump directly to a topic.

Explore with an open mind

Observe and ask

Astronomy begins with observations. Light from distant objects carries clues about their temperature, motion and composition.

Keep discovering

New missions and instruments add to what we know. Scientific explanations can become more detailed as fresh evidence arrives.
