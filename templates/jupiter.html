Solar System / Jupiter

The giant of the Solar System

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System. This gas giant has deep cloud bands, powerful storms and a family of fascinating moons.

PositionFifth planet from the Sun
Planet typeGas giant
Notable featureThe Great Red Spot

A planet without a solid surface

Jupiter is made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Its colourful visible clouds sit high in a deep atmosphere, and there is no solid surface like Earth's for a spacecraft to land on.

A miniature system of moons

Jupiter has many moons, including the four large Galilean moons: Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto. Each is a distinct world, and some may have conditions of interest to scientists studying oceans beyond Earth.

Radio emissions from Ganymede

During a 2021 flyby of Jupiter's moon Ganymede, NASA's Juno spacecraft measured radio emissions that have been converted to sound. Radio waves travel through space; ordinary sound does not.
