Solar System / The Sun

Our nearest star

The Sun

The Sun is the star at the centre of our Solar System. Its light and heat reach every planet, while its gravity holds the system together.

Object typeStar
AgeAbout 4.6 billion years
Light to EarthAbout 8 minutes 20 seconds

A star, not a planet

The Sun is a huge sphere of hot plasma. Deep in its core, hydrogen nuclei combine to form helium and release energy. That energy travels outward and eventually leaves the Sun as sunlight and other forms of radiation.

The centre of our planetary system

The Sun's gravity keeps planets, dwarf planets and many smaller objects in orbit. Its energy also drives processes in planetary atmospheres and helps make Earth's surface environment suitable for life.
