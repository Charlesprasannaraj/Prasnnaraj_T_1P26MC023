Solar System / Mars

The red planet

Mars

Mars is a cold, rocky world with a thin atmosphere, towering volcanoes and landscapes that preserve clues to a wetter past.

PositionFourth planet from the Sun
Length of a dayAbout 24 hours 37 minutes
Natural moonsTwo: Phobos and Deimos

A world of ancient landscapes

Mars has a rusty colour because iron-rich minerals in its soil have oxidised. It is home to enormous volcanoes, deep canyons, impact craters and polar ice. Features on the surface show that liquid water flowed there in the distant past.

Why missions visit Mars

Orbiters map the planet and study its atmosphere, while landers and rovers examine the surface directly. These missions help scientists understand how Mars changed over time and whether ancient environments could once have supported microbial life.

Listen to Mars

NASA's InSight seismometer captured vibrations from Martian wind moving the lander's solar panels. This version is raised two octaves to make the data audible; it is not ordinary airborne sound.
